STEP 1. Years: YEARS = [this year, last year] (in 2026: 2026 and 2025; in 2027: 2027 and 2026). FEC data is organized by two-year cycle, where a year's cycle is the year rounded up to even (2025 and 2026 are both cycle 2026; 2027 is cycle 2028). CYCLES = the distinct cycles of YEARS. Query each cycle in CYCLES and keep only transactions whose date falls in YEARS.

STEP 2. Read 'Competitor list'!A4:G200. Keep rows whose Tier (C) is "Direct" or "Lead source". For each: Vendor (A), Tier (C), Category (D), Match pattern (G, a JavaScript regex tested on the upper-cased recipient name). Search terms for the FEC recipient_name search (lowercase): use this map, and for any vendor not in it, use the vendor name lowercased with any text in parentheses removed:
